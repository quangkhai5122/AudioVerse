# Competitor positioning and evidence register

**As-of:** 2026-11-17. Competing offerings can change. No claim of market share, retention or price parity without current verified data.

## Vietnam audio library
Fonos (licensed audiobooks and podcasts), Voiz FM (audiobooks, creators), Waka (text+audio), niche audio-story apps. Strengths: rights, content library, narration quality, established consumer familiarity. AudioVerse should NOT compete only by number of books or cheaper TTS.

## Interactive AI fiction
AI Dungeon (world info/memory systems), StoryVerse (interactive AI audiobook experience), Vietnamese AI storytelling/roleplay platforms such as Xomi/Truyện Nhập Vai. Strengths: prompt flexibility, brand/community, or character chat. AudioVerse should validate authored state graph, narrative continuity, fast audio handoff, high-quality Vietnamese acting and licensing.

## Reader/TTS utilities
User-owned-file text-to-speech tools, EPUB-to-audio apps and OS accessibility narration. Existing reader products prove "paste link to story and AI reads" is not a sufficient moat. AudioVerse primarily curates/licences *end-user entertainment*, not a generic document reader.

## Competitor audit protocol
Every 2 months, a human tester should score a real device and trial account on: audio quality (30-minute listen), offline/access, episode navigation, paid plan transparency, genre coverage, interactive consequences, LLM consistency after 20 turns, error handling, licensing evidence, store-review friction. Record version/date/geography, paid/free tier and screenshot internally; never present a hypothetical feature as verified competitor implementation.

## Explicit falsifiable hypotheses
H1: Strongly authored, consequential branching retains mystery listeners better than unconstrained AI chat. H2: Using curated human intros increases completion vs synthetic-only intros. H3: A seamless passive default outperforms mandatory interaction for listeners before sleep/while commuting. H4: Different voice per character increases enjoyment but may also increase latency/cost. Test controlled alternatives.
