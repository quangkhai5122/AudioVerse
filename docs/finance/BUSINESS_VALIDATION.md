# Consumer research, acquisition and business validation

## Preliminary segment
Adult Vietnamese fans of mystery/horror audio; age segment and accessibility need testing. Recruit lawfully through existing book communities and permissioned creator audiences. Competitors include Spotify/YouTube listening patterns, audiobooks and interactive roleplay apps, not only paid premium audio products.

## Five falsifiable claims
H1: Human/curated opening narration increases completion vs robotic synthetic. H2: Optional choice creates greater enjoyment than mandatory choice. H3: Story graph prevents contradictions enough to improve satisfaction vs free-form chatbot. H4: A serialized mystery prompts >1 voluntary session. H5: Users pay for a finished next season more readily than for generic AI tokens.

## Validation plan
Week-style research stages independent of calendar dates: 1) 12 in-depth interviews about listening behavior and alternatives; 2) 30-person usability of original audio story; 3) 100–300-person invite beta under lawful deployment; 4) real purchase intention after legal app-store setup and approved pricing. Avoid vanity survey "would you pay" as substitute for completed purchase.

## Instrumentation
Track install -> first play -> 5min -> first choice -> chapter finish -> next-story start -> D7 -> paywall view -> purchase; separate fixed vs interactive. Collect consented feedback on voice fatigue, pacing, route impact, price fairness and interruptions. Analytics must be privacy-safe and disabled until implementation reviewed.

## Distribution concepts
Creator cross-promotion only under agreement; short *authorized original* teaser clips on TikTok/YouTube; community reviews, early-access audio episodes, internal referral tokens. Do not distribute infringing story clips to promote the product.

## Stop / pivot signals
Unacceptable legal classification/licensing cost; no rights-cleared content supply; <reasonable independently set threshold> of beta users hear beyond first episode; interaction causes higher abandonment than passive; AI cost per paid user inconsistent with contribution margin. Choose numeric targets after pilot baseline rather than invent market facts.
