# ADR-007: Independent rights scopes for text, performance and AI voice
**Status:** Accepted for starter **Date:** 2026-11-17

## Decision
Represent stream, offline, derivative, TTS, voice_clone and training as distinct authorizations. Each third-party work needs chain-of-title evidence; each voice clone needs signed performer/voice authorization, model weight rights and purpose restrictions. No self-attestation auto-approval.

## Why
A public YouTube audio link is not a license; a streaming license is not a transformative or model-training license. Separate rights let product offer authorized passive audio without falsely implying interactive rights.

## Follow-up
T002 builds legal metadata, reviewer pipeline and restrictions at ingest/generation/delivery.
